Workflow

Structured stages, never one-shot prompts.

Each stage emits inspectable artifacts that must pass human review before downstream assets are generated.

  1. Rights-cleared source indexing: Decompose cleared narrative material into canonical scene graphs, character state ledgers, and timeline constraints.
  2. Grounded research packets: Assemble period, architectural, costume, and acoustic references anchored to source citations.
  3. Shot & sequence planning: Build deterministic scene manifests, continuity tables, and pacing budgets before touching audiovisual synthesis.
  4. Human review gates: Require explicit creative approval at script, storyboard, and rough-assembly checkpoints so drift is caught early.
  5. Deterministic assembly & provenance: Compile approved shots, audio stems, and edit timelines with reproducible build manifests.
Design principles

Why it stays deliberate.

Rights clarity first

ProductionPipeline is built strictly for public-domain, original, or explicitly licensed narrative works. Provenance tracking records which source passages and approved references feed every scene.

Private development status

ProductionPipeline is developed internally under Goza Labs and does not have a public source repository. For collaboration or research questions, email rogiegoza@gmail.com.

AutoShot

A free desktop utility for Windows and Linux that presses an arrow key or scrolls a window, captures a screenshot after each step, and stops automatically when the page stops changing. Supports full-window, content-area, full-screen, or custom drawn regions in PNG, JPG, PDF, and more.

Kinaiya

A local-first AI platform pairing expressive real-time voice with an independent agent for useful work. Research scope includes shared-transcript coordination, adaptive executable harnesses, and low-latency voice interaction.
